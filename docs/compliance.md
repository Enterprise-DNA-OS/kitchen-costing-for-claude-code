# Selected receiving record checks

Reviewed 28 September 2026. This costing tool checks a narrow set of Australian receiving records. It does not certify a kitchen, release food or replace an approved food safety plan. The responsible operator reviews every finding and the underlying evidence.

## AU-RECEIVE

[FSANZ receiving food guidance](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/receiving-food), explaining Standard 3.2.2 clause 5, describes temperature controls for incoming potentially hazardous food. This build flags chilled receipts with no temperature or above 5C, hot receipts with no temperature or below 60C, and frozen receipts without confirmation that they arrived frozen hard. A documented safe alternative needs human review. Entering a method reference does not automatically pass or release anything. Held and rejected deliveries remain visible and do not increase available stock.

Storage category alone is a conservative screening assumption. An operator must classify their ingredients and applicable controls. Not all chilled products are potentially hazardous. Missing readings are record gaps, not proof of unsafe food.

## AU-SUPPLIER

[FSANZ food traceability guidance](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/food-traceability) describes identifying received food and the supplier's name and Australian address. The check flags blank supplier addresses. The receiving register preserves the ingredient, supplier, batch, time, operator and disposition. It does not validate the address or provide a complete recall system.

## Scope and business policies

FSANZ Chapter 3 applies in Australia. A site outside Australia gets a scope finding and requires its own reviewed rules. Seven-day stock counts, recipe margin targets and supplier review dates are operator policies. Invoice due dates come from entered terms. They are not statutory defaults.

No command approves a food disposition, guarantees an allergen statement or proves regulatory compliance. Allergen notes are ordinary operator-entered records. This tool does not implement food safety training, all temperature monitoring, recall execution or retention schedules. Keep those processes in the business's food safety plan.
